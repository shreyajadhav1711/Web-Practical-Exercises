The Wonders of Space Exploration

Space exploration is the ongoing discovery and exploration of celestial structures in outer space by means of continuously evolving space technology. While the study of space is carried out mainly by astronomers with telescopes, the physical exploration of space is conducted both by unmanned robotic space probes and human spaceflight.

Entering the space race allowed humanity to walk on the Moon, send rovers to Mars, and position powerful satellites into orbit to look back at our own planet. These advancements continue to inspire new generations of scientists, engineers, and programmers who build the software that powers modern spaceships.

Key Space Agencies

  • ESA (Europe)
  • ISRO (India)

Steps to Launch a Rocket

  1. Payload integration and final safety checks.
  2. Fueling the liquid propellant stages.
  3. Final countdown and engine ignition sequence.
  4. Liftoff and atmospheric stage separation.
